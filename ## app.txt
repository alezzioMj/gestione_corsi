## Prefetching 
Is the process of loading routes in background before the user navigates in.

-Static Route: The full route is prefetched
-Dynamic route: Prefetching is skipper totally or partially.

## Streaming

With streaming, the server sends pieces of the page as they become ready,
instead of waiting for the entire page to finish rendering.

Next.js implements this automatically with `loading.tsx`: behind the scenes,
it wraps the page content in a `<Suspense>` boundary, using `loading.tsx`
as the fallback UI (e.g. a spinner). The fallback is shown immediately,
and gets swapped for the real content once it's ready.
